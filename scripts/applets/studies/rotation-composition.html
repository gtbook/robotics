Each editor uses the yaw–pitch–roll convention R = Rz(ψ) Ry(θ) Rx(φ).

R₀₁ · Frame 1 to frame 0

Drag for yaw and pitch. Scroll for roll.

R₁₂ · Frame 2 to frame 1

Drag for yaw and pitch. Scroll for roll.

Frame transformation

p₀ = R₀₁ R₁₂ p₂

R₁₂ maps p₂ into frame 1. R₀₁ then maps that result into frame 0.

Reversed product

p′ = R₁₂ R₀₁ p₂

The numerical comparison applies R₀₁ to the entries of p₂ from frame 2. As a coordinate map, R₀₁ takes a vector expressed in frame 1.

The norm ‖p₀ − p′‖ is the Euclidean distance between the two numerical outputs.
