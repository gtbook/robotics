Rear-axle body frame

World frame
dporigin/dt = vcar · x̂B

Under the no-slip assumption, lateral velocity is zero at the rear-axle midpoint. A point at longitudinal offset Δx from the axle has lateral velocity (dθ/dt) Δx in the body frame.

Ackermann steering

Body frame
R = L / tan φ

In a no-slip turn, the wheel velocities are tangent to circles with a common instantaneous center on the rear-axle line. The inner front wheel has a greater steering angle in magnitude. R is the signed turning radius at the rear-axle midpoint.

Differential kinematics

Body frame
dθ/dt = (vcar / L) · tan φ, dφ/dt = u2

The inputs are rear-axle speed vcar and steering rate u2. At the virtual front wheel, the lateral velocity vcar tan φ equals L dθ/dt. The plot uses a separate vertical scale for each variable.
