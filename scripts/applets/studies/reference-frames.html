Camera observations

pC = RCW(pW − tWC)

To express a fixed landmark in the camera frame, subtract the camera’s world position tWC and rotate by RCW. The shaded sector marks the field of view.

Planar arm kinematics

TWE(q) = TWB TBE(q)

Inverse kinematics solves for joint angles q = (q₁, q₂) along the specified end-effector path. Composing the base pose TWB with TBE(q) gives the end-effector pose in the world frame.

Vehicle pose on terrain

TWB = (RWB, pWB)

The horizontal path and yaw are prescribed. Terrain heights at the wheels determine chassis height, roll, and pitch. The wheel readouts report the vertical separation between each suspension mount and wheel center.

Lengths are in arbitrary units.
